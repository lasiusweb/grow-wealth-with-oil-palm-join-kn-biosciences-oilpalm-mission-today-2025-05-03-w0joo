About KN Biosciences

Founded in 2015, KN Biosciences emerged from a vision to revolutionize India's agricultural landscape through sustainable oil palm cultivation. Our journey began with a small team of agricultural experts and has grown into a comprehensive support ecosystem for farmers across multiple states.

Our mission is to empower farmers with scientific knowledge, modern technology, and reliable market access while promoting environmental sustainability. We believe in creating lasting partnerships that benefit both farmers and the planet, guided by our core values of integrity, innovation, and farmer-first approach.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum land requirement for oil palm cultivation?

We recommend a minimum of 2 hectares for optimal returns, though we work with farmers having as little as 1 hectare. Land suitability assessment is provided free of charge.

How long does it take for the first harvest?

Initial harvest begins in the 4th year, with full production achieved by the 7th year. We provide complete support and interim crop planning during the gestation period.

What kind of financial support is available?

Farmers can access government subsidies up to ₹2 lakhs per hectare, plus additional financial assistance through our banking partners. We help with all paperwork and applications.

How does the buy-back program work?

We guarantee purchase of all produce at minimum 10% above market rates, with payment within 48 hours of delivery. Transportation and logistics support is included.

What technical support do you provide?

Our package includes soil testing, irrigation planning, monthly field visits, 24/7 expert consultation, and access to our digital monitoring platform. Training workshops are conducted regularly.
